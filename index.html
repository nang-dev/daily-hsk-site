Designed from the memory research, not the other way round

Daily HSK's schedule and exercises follow findings that hold up across decades of studies:

  • Spaced repetition with expanding gaps (Cepeda et al., 2008).
  • Retrieval practice — testing beats re-reading (Roediger & Karpicke, 2006).
  • Interleaving word types and skills instead of blocking (Kornell & Bjork, 2008).
  • Immediate corrective feedback on every answer (Metcalfe, 2017).
  • Words in context — meaning learned through sentences, not lists alone (Webb, 2007).

Full reading list is inside the app under Settings → Learning science.
